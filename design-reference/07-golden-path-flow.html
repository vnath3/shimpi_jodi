The Golden Path

One successful journey — from a PDF on an admin's desk to two families talking

Region admin System Member A (Priya) Member B (Rahul)
1

Getting the profile in

admin side
Region admin · Marathwada
Uploads a batch of 12 biodata PDFs collected from the samaj
Scoped to their region only — they can't see or touch other regions' data
import_batches
System · extraction worker
Reads each PDF, maps it to the profile schema, scores confidence per field
Runs off the main app. If it fails, nothing user-facing breaks
extracted_drafts · confidence jsonb
Region admin
Reviews the queue — checks only the amber-flagged fields against the PDF, fixes two, approves
~40 seconds per profile instead of re-typing everything
status → approved
Consent gate
Approval only sends a claim link. The profile stays unlisted and invisible until Priya publishes it herself. The admin never publishes on her behalf.

She claims and publishes

~90 seconds
Priya
Opens the claim link on her phone, enters her email, gets a 6-digit code
auth_otps
Priya
Sees her details already filled in — corrects her height, confirms the rest
Every field is a dropdown, so filters stay clean
profiles · profile_family
Priya
Adds 4 photos and a 40-second video introduction; answers 3 prompts
Video is capped at 60s and transcoded to an HLS ladder in the background
media_assets → media_variants
Priya
Sets location: lives in Pune, works in Pune, hometown Nashik — open to move, prefers Pune or Nashik
Four separate facts, because "where she is" and "where she'll settle" are different questions
profile_location_prefs
Priya
Ticks the two required consents, publishes. Profile strength: 92%
consents (append-only) · status → published
3

He finds her

next morning
System · 8:00 AM
Builds Rahul's Daily Five from the candidate pool
Same community, opposite gender, not blocked, no prior interest — enforced in one function so the rules can't drift
candidates_for(profile_id)
Rahul
Sees Priya's card — photo, three facts, one prompt answer, and honest compatibility chips
Blurhash paints instantly; the 720px card image streams in behind it
Rahul
Opens the full profile, watches her video intro, reads the family section
profile_views (partitioned)
Rahul
Sends interest, tagged "Our families seem aligned"
DB trigger verifies same community + opposite gender before the row is written
interests · status pending
4

She says yes

same evening
Priya
Sees the interest with its intent tag, opens his profile, accepts
Had she declined, he'd never be told — silent decline matters in a small community
status → accepted · conversations created (locked)
The shell opens. The pearl appears.
Both sides see it. This is the app's emotional peak — and the only place the logo animates.
5

The conversation opens

the paywall
Paywall — placed after the yes, never before
Browsing and expressing interest stay free forever. You charge at the moment of proven value, not to unlock the front door.
Rahul
Picks the 6-month plan, pays ₹799 by UPI
One-time payment, no auto-renewal, no card stored — stated on the screen
payments → subscriptions
System
Unlocks the conversation for both of them
Priya doesn't have to pay too — one payment opens one conversation for both sides
conversations · status → open
Rahul → Priya
First messages exchanged, with the safety banner at the top of the thread
messages
