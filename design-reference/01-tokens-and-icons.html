Logo directions

Option A — Wordmark only
Shimpi Jodi
Option B — Wordmark + mark
Shimpi Jodi

Option B's mark: two threads meeting and parting — a quiet nod to the Shimpi (tailoring) heritage without spelling it out, and to two lives joining. Never used standalone as a "crest" — always paired with the wordmark, keeping the tribute and heritage subtle as discussed.

Signature element

The interlocking thread
This curved two-line motif recurs subtly across the app — as a loading indicator, a divider between profile sections, and the "match" moment animation — so the brand identity is felt in motion, not just in the static logo.
